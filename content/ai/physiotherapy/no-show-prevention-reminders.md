# No-Show Prevention & Reminders for Physiotherapy Clinics

No-show prevention for a physiotherapy clinic is an automated system that confirms appointments, sends a reminder sequence, and fills cancellations from a waitlist — with particular attention to sessions within an active treatment plan, where a missed visit can set back a patient's whole recovery timeline. It matters because physiotherapy schedules run on frequent, recurring sessions, which means more reminder touchpoints and more opportunities for a missed one to go unnoticed.

## How It Works
Input: Scheduled sessions from the practice management system, patient contact preferences, historical confirmation and cancellation data
Processing: The system sends a multi-touch reminder sequence, tracks confirmations, and triggers waitlist outreach the moment a slot cancels
Output: Higher confirmation rates, filled last-minute openings, and visibility into which reminder pattern actually works for this patient base

## Use Cases
- Running a multi-touch reminder sequence across a recurring, multi-session treatment schedule
- Automatically offering a cancelled slot to the next eligible patient on a waitlist
- Flagging patients with a pattern of missed sessions for a different, more proactive confirmation approach
- Tracking confirmation rates by reminder channel to see what this patient base actually responds to
- Giving front-desk staff a live view of which sessions are confirmed versus still pending

## Benefits
- Recovers treatment time that would otherwise sit empty on a no-show
- Reduces the daily manual work of confirming a high volume of recurring sessions
- Turns last-minute cancellations into filled slots instead of lost revenue
- Helps keep patients on schedule with the recovery timeline their treatment plan depends on

## FAQ
### Does a high-frequency treatment schedule need a different reminder approach than a one-off appointment?
Yes — more frequent sessions mean more reminder touchpoints, and the system is built to handle that volume without becoming repetitive or ignorable to patients.

### Does this replace confirmation calls entirely?
For most patients, text and automated confirmation covers it. Patients who don't respond, or who have a no-show history, can still be flagged for a personal call.

### Can it fill a cancellation the same day it happens?
Yes — the moment a slot opens, the system can work down a waitlist by text or call rather than waiting for staff to notice the gap.

### Will patients get annoyed by too many reminders?
The sequence is tuned to the number of touches shown to improve confirmation without becoming noise, and stops as soon as a patient confirms.
