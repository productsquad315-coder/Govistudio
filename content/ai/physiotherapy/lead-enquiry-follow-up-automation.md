# Lead & Enquiry Follow-Up Automation for Physiotherapy Clinics

Lead follow-up automation for a physiotherapy clinic is a system that follows up on new-patient enquiries and, critically, on patients mid-treatment-plan who haven't booked their next session — instead of relying on staff to track it manually. It matters because physiotherapy outcomes depend on completing a course of care, and a patient who quietly stops rebooking is both a lost patient and, often, an incomplete recovery.

## How It Works
Input: Website enquiry forms, treatment-plan data showing sessions completed versus prescribed, missed-call callback lists
Processing: The system applies a follow-up sequence tailored to where the patient is in their plan, and stops automatically once they rebook or respond
Output: Booked sessions, completed treatment plans, or a clearly flagged list of patients who need a personal call

## Use Cases
- Following up automatically when a patient hasn't booked their next session in an active treatment plan
- Re-engaging website enquiries that never received a response within the first hour
- Working a list of missed-call leads with a structured text/call sequence
- Flagging patients who've gone quiet mid-plan for a personal check-in call rather than an automated message
- Reporting which lead sources and referral types actually convert to completed treatment plans

## Benefits
- Recovers session revenue from treatment plans that would otherwise go incomplete
- Improves patient outcomes by keeping people on track with their prescribed course of care
- Removes follow-up from depending on one staff member remembering to check
- Gives the practice visibility into where in a treatment plan patients tend to drop off

## FAQ
### How does it know a patient is mid-treatment-plan and not just a one-off visit?
It reads the treatment-plan and session data recorded in your practice management system to distinguish a course of care from a single appointment.

### Does it stop once a patient responds?
Yes. The sequence stops the moment a patient books, replies, or asks to be left alone.

### Can this detect drop-off automatically?
Yes, if session and treatment-plan data is recorded in the practice management system (WebPT, Cliniko, Jane App), the system can flag when a patient hasn't rebooked within the expected interval.

### Will this feel pushy to patients?
The cadence and tone are built with the practice, and the message is a check-in on care they've already started — not persistent pressure, and it stops on any response.
