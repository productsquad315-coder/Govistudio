# AI Systems for Cosmetic Clinics

AI systems for cosmetic clinics are custom-built software that automates patient communication, scheduling, and front-desk workflows around the booking and EMR platform a clinic already runs — Vagaro, Mangomint, Zenoti, Boulevard, or similar — instead of replacing it. Cosmetic clinics compete heavily on responsiveness (fast reply on treatment enquiries) and repeat-visit cadence (Botox and filler touch-ups on a fixed interval), and these systems are built around both.

## How It Works
Input: Missed calls, web enquiry forms, booking and treatment-history data from the clinic's platform, and patient communication history
Processing: AI evaluates the request, checks the schedule, applies the clinic's own booking and follow-up rules, and drafts or sends the appropriate response
Output: Booked appointments, confirmed reminders, recovered leads, and updated records back in the clinic's booking platform

## Use Cases
- Answering and following up on missed calls and web enquiries about treatments and pricing
- Following up automatically on consultations that didn't convert to a booked treatment
- Sending appointment reminders and collecting confirmations for treatment appointments
- Reminding patients when they're approaching their typical touch-up interval for Botox, filler, or similar treatments
- Syncing booked and cancelled appointments back into Vagaro, Mangomint, Zenoti, or Boulevard automatically

## Benefits
- Recovers revenue lost to missed calls and consultations that never convert to a booked treatment
- Reduces no-show rates on treatment appointments
- Frees front-desk staff from repetitive phone and follow-up work during high enquiry volume
- Keeps the booking platform as the single source of truth — no duplicate data entry
- Turns one-time treatments into a repeat, scheduled maintenance relationship

## FAQ
### Does an AI receptionist replace my front-desk team?
No. It handles overflow and after-hours contact — missed calls, routine scheduling questions, confirmations — and hands anything requiring judgment or a human touch to your staff.

### Will it work with the booking software we already use?
Yes. These systems are built to integrate with the major cosmetic and medspa platforms — Vagaro, Mangomint, Zenoti, and Boulevard — rather than requiring a switch.

### How does this help with speed-to-response on new enquiries?
Cosmetic enquiries tend to convert best when answered fast — the system responds immediately to calls and web enquiries instead of leaving them for a callback later in the day.

### Is patient data handled securely?
Yes. Systems are built with appropriate data handling, encryption, and access controls, and integrate with your existing platform rather than creating a separate, unsecured copy of patient data.

### What's the fastest place to start?
Consultation follow-up and missed-call recovery typically show impact first, since unconverted consults and unanswered enquiries are direct, measurable losses.
