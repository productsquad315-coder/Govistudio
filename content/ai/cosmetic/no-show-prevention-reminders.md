# No-Show Prevention & Reminders for Cosmetic Clinics

No-show prevention for a cosmetic clinic is an automated system that confirms treatment appointments, sends a reminder sequence, and fills cancellations from a waitlist — reducing the empty treatment-room time that's especially costly given how much provider and room capacity cosmetic treatments consume per booking. It matters because a missed treatment slot isn't just a lost appointment, it's often a lost provider-hour that's expensive to leave unfilled.

## How It Works
Input: Scheduled treatment appointments from the booking platform, patient contact preferences, historical confirmation and cancellation data
Processing: The system sends a multi-touch reminder sequence, tracks confirmations, and triggers waitlist outreach the moment a slot cancels
Output: Higher confirmation rates, filled last-minute openings, and visibility into which reminder pattern actually works for this patient base

## Use Cases
- Running a multi-touch reminder sequence ahead of treatment appointments
- Automatically offering a cancelled treatment slot to the next eligible patient on a waitlist
- Flagging patients with a pattern of no-shows for a different, more proactive confirmation approach
- Tracking confirmation rates by treatment type and reminder channel
- Giving front-desk staff a live view of which appointments are confirmed versus still pending

## Benefits
- Recovers provider and treatment-room time that would otherwise sit empty on a no-show
- Reduces the daily manual work of confirming a full treatment schedule
- Turns last-minute cancellations into filled slots instead of lost revenue
- Protects margin on treatments where provider time is the most expensive input

## FAQ
### Why does a no-show cost more in a cosmetic clinic than some other practices?
Cosmetic treatments often require a specific provider and room for a set block of time, so an empty slot is a full block of unused, expensive capacity rather than a short, easily-absorbed gap.

### Does this replace confirmation calls entirely?
For most patients, text and automated confirmation covers it. Patients who don't respond, or who have a no-show history, can still be flagged for a personal call.

### Can it fill a cancelled slot the same day?
Yes — the moment a slot opens, the system can work down a waitlist by text or call rather than waiting for staff to notice the gap.

### Will patients get annoyed by too many reminders?
The sequence is tuned to the number of touches shown to improve confirmation without becoming noise, and stops as soon as a patient confirms.
