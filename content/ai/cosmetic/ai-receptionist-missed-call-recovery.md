# AI Receptionist & Missed-Call Recovery for Cosmetic Clinics

An AI receptionist for a cosmetic clinic answers or immediately follows up on calls and web enquiries the front desk can't take fast enough, and books directly against real treatment-room availability. It matters because cosmetic enquiries are highly price- and time-sensitive — a prospective patient who doesn't get a fast answer on availability or pricing often books with the next clinic they find instead of waiting for a callback.

## How It Works
Input: Inbound calls, voicemails, and web enquiry forms, current treatment-room and provider availability from the booking platform
Processing: The system answers or responds immediately, identifies the treatment of interest, shares relevant pricing information, and checks the schedule
Output: A booked consultation or treatment, a routed message to the right staff member, or a recovered lead that would otherwise have gone cold

## Use Cases
- Capturing new-patient enquiries that call or message during peak enquiry periods
- Booking consultations and treatment appointments directly against real-time availability
- Answering common questions (treatment pricing ranges, provider availability, location) without tying up a staff member
- Following up on same-day web enquiries before the prospective patient looks elsewhere
- Logging every enquiry outcome back into the clinic's booking platform

## Benefits
- Recovers enquiries that would otherwise go to a competing clinic or medspa within minutes
- Removes the "stuck on one line, missed the next enquiry" problem on a single front-desk phone
- Extends effective coverage without extending staff hours
- Reduces interruptions to providers and injectors currently fielding overflow calls between appointments

## FAQ
### Can it quote pricing on specific treatments?
It can share the pricing ranges and information the clinic provides, and route anything requiring a personalized consultation quote to a booked consult.

### Does it sound like a real person or an obvious bot?
It's built to handle the routine share of enquiries (pricing questions, availability, booking) smoothly and hand off anything unusual to a human.

### How fast does it respond to a web enquiry?
Immediately — the goal is to respond before a time-sensitive cosmetic enquiry moves on to the next clinic in their search.

### Can it text patients instead of calling them back?
Yes — for cosmetic enquiries especially, a text follow-up often converts better and faster than a voicemail callback.
