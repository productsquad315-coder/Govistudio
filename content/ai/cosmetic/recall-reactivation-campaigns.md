# Recall & Reactivation Campaigns for Cosmetic Clinics

A recall and reactivation system for a cosmetic clinic automatically identifies patients approaching their typical touch-up interval — Botox, filler, and similar treatments that wear off on a predictable schedule — and runs structured outreach to get them rebooked before they consider a different clinic. It matters because touch-up recall is one of the most reliable repeat-revenue opportunities a cosmetic clinic has, and it's also the easiest to lose to a competing clinic if the patient isn't reminded before the treatment visibly wears off.

## How It Works
Input: Treatment-history data from the booking platform, typical touch-up intervals by treatment type
Processing: The system tracks each patient's treatment date and expected interval, and triggers outreach as the patient approaches or passes it
Output: Rebooked touch-up appointments, a reactivated patient list, and visibility into how many patients returned versus went elsewhere

## Use Cases
- Reaching out to patients as they approach their typical Botox or filler touch-up interval
- Reactivating patients who are overdue and haven't rebooked
- Filling schedule gaps directly from the recall-due list
- Applying a distinct win-back approach for long-lapsed patients versus recently-overdue ones
- Reporting recall-to-rebooked conversion by treatment type

## Benefits
- Protects the clinic's most predictable, recurring revenue stream from quietly going to a competitor
- Fills treatment-room time that would otherwise sit open
- Reaches patients before the treatment visibly wears off and they start looking elsewhere
- Removes recall management from being a manual, easily-deprioritized front-desk task

## FAQ
### How does the system know when a specific patient is due for a touch-up?
It tracks the date and type of their last treatment against the typical interval for that treatment, and triggers outreach as they approach it.

### What if a patient's actual interval differs from the typical one?
Thresholds are set with the clinic and can account for treatment type and any patient-specific pattern reflected in their booking history.

### Can this run alongside our existing recall process?
Yes — it's built around your existing treatment-history data in the booking platform, so it strengthens the process already in place.

### Does reactivation outreach need to go through a licensed provider?
Routine scheduling outreach doesn't require clinical judgment and can be automated; anything involving treatment advice is routed to the clinic's own provider staff.
