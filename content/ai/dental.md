# AI Systems for Dental Clinics

AI systems for dental clinics are custom-built software that automates patient communication, scheduling, and front-desk workflows around the tools a dental practice already runs on — Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental, or Curve Dental — instead of replacing them. They handle the repetitive parts of running a front desk (answering calls, chasing confirmations, following up on treatment plans) so practice staff spend less time on admin and more time with patients.

## How It Works
Input: Missed calls, web enquiry forms, appointment data from the practice management system, and patient communication history
Processing: AI evaluates the request, checks the schedule, applies the practice's own booking and follow-up rules, and drafts or sends the appropriate response
Output: Booked appointments, confirmed reminders, recovered leads, and updated records back in the practice management system

## Use Cases
- Answering and following up on missed calls during business hours and after close, when most dental front desks can't pick up
- Following up automatically on treatment plans that were presented but not scheduled
- Sending appointment reminders and collecting confirmations without staff manually calling each patient
- Reactivating patients who are overdue for a recall or hygiene visit
- Syncing booked and cancelled appointments back into Dentrix, Eaglesoft, or Open Dental automatically

## Benefits
- Recovers revenue currently lost to missed calls and unscheduled treatment plans
- Reduces no-show rates, which average around 15% industry-wide and cost practices tens of thousands of dollars a year in empty chair time
- Frees front-desk staff from repetitive phone and follow-up work
- Keeps the practice management system as the single source of truth — no duplicate data entry
- Scales patient communication without adding headcount as the practice grows

## FAQ
### Does an AI receptionist replace my front-desk team?
No. It handles overflow and after-hours contact — missed calls, routine scheduling questions, confirmations — and hands anything requiring judgment or a human touch to your staff.

### Will it work with the practice management software we already use?
Yes. These systems are built to integrate with the major dental platforms — Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental, and Curve Dental — rather than requiring a switch.

### How is this different from a general answering service?
A general answering service takes a message. An AI system checks your actual schedule, applies your practice's booking rules, and can complete the task — booking, confirming, or following up — without a callback loop.

### Is patient data handled securely?
Yes. Systems are built with HIPAA-appropriate data handling, encryption, and access controls, and integrate with your practice management system rather than creating a separate, unsecured copy of patient data.

### What's the fastest place to start?
Missed-call recovery and no-show prevention typically show impact first, since they address the most direct source of lost revenue — unanswered calls and empty chairs.
