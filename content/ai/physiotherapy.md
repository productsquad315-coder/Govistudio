# AI Systems for Physiotherapy Clinics

AI systems for physiotherapy clinics are custom-built software that automates patient communication, scheduling, and front-desk workflows around the practice management platform a clinic already runs — WebPT, Cliniko, Jane App, or similar — instead of replacing it. Physiotherapy runs on multi-visit treatment plans rather than one-off appointments, so keeping patients engaged across a full course of care is a bigger factor than in most other clinic types.

## How It Works
Input: Missed calls, web enquiry forms, treatment-plan and appointment data from the practice management system, and patient communication history
Processing: AI evaluates the request, checks the schedule, applies the practice's own booking and follow-up rules, and drafts or sends the appropriate response
Output: Booked appointments, confirmed reminders, recovered leads, and updated records back in the practice management system

## Use Cases
- Answering and following up on missed calls during business hours and after close
- Following up automatically when a patient hasn't rebooked the next session in an active treatment plan
- Sending appointment reminders and collecting confirmations across a multi-session course of care
- Reactivating patients who paused treatment before completing their plan
- Syncing booked and cancelled appointments back into WebPT, Cliniko, or Jane App automatically

## Benefits
- Recovers revenue lost to missed calls and incomplete treatment plans
- Reduces no-show and treatment-dropout rates through consistent, multi-session follow-up
- Frees front-desk staff from repetitive phone and rebooking work
- Keeps the practice management system as the single source of truth — no duplicate data entry
- Helps patients actually complete the treatment plan their outcome depends on

## FAQ
### Does an AI receptionist replace my front-desk team?
No. It handles overflow and after-hours contact — missed calls, routine scheduling questions, confirmations — and hands anything requiring judgment or a human touch to your staff.

### Will it work with the practice management software we already use?
Yes. These systems are built to integrate with the major physiotherapy and allied health platforms — WebPT, Cliniko, Jane App, and similar — rather than requiring a switch.

### How does this help with treatment-plan drop-off specifically?
The system can track where a patient is in their prescribed course of care and follow up automatically when the next session isn't booked, rather than relying on staff to notice a gap.

### Is patient data handled securely?
Yes. Systems are built with appropriate data handling, encryption, and access controls, and integrate with your practice management system rather than creating a separate, unsecured copy of patient data.

### What's the fastest place to start?
Treatment-plan follow-up and no-show prevention typically show impact first, since incomplete courses of care are both a revenue loss and a patient-outcome issue.
