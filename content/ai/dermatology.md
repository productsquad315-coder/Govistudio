# AI Systems for Dermatology Clinics

AI systems for dermatology clinics are custom-built software that automates patient communication, scheduling, and front-desk workflows around the EHR and practice management platform a clinic already runs — Nextech, ModMed, DermEngine, or similar — instead of replacing it. Dermatology practices run a mix of medical and cosmetic visit types with different booking, insurance, and follow-up needs, and these systems are built around that mix rather than treating every visit the same.

## How It Works
Input: Missed calls, web enquiry forms, appointment and visit-type data from the EHR/practice management system, and patient communication history
Processing: AI evaluates the request, distinguishes medical from cosmetic visit types, checks the schedule, and applies the practice's own booking and follow-up rules
Output: Booked appointments, confirmed reminders, recovered leads, and updated records back in the practice's EHR

## Use Cases
- Answering and following up on missed calls, routing medical concerns differently from cosmetic consult requests
- Following up automatically on cosmetic consultations that didn't convert to a booked procedure
- Sending appointment reminders and collecting confirmations for both medical and cosmetic visit types
- Reactivating patients overdue for a skin-check or follow-up visit
- Syncing booked and cancelled appointments back into Nextech, ModMed, or DermEngine automatically

## Benefits
- Recovers revenue lost to missed calls and cosmetic consultations that never convert
- Reduces no-show rates across both medical and elective visit types
- Frees front-desk staff from repetitive phone and follow-up work during high call-volume periods
- Keeps the EHR as the single source of truth — no duplicate data entry across a separate booking tool
- Handles the medical/cosmetic visit-type split without staff manually routing every enquiry

## FAQ
### Can it tell the difference between a medical concern and a cosmetic consult request?
Yes — the system is built to route based on the nature of the enquiry, since medical concerns and elective cosmetic bookings typically need different handling, timing, and staff involvement.

### Does it work with the EHR our practice already uses?
Yes. These systems are built to integrate with the major dermatology platforms — Nextech, ModMed, and DermEngine — rather than requiring a switch.

### How is this different from a general answering service?
A general answering service takes a message. An AI system checks your actual schedule, applies your practice's booking rules, and can complete the task — booking, confirming, or following up — without a callback loop.

### Is patient data handled securely?
Yes. Systems are built with HIPAA-appropriate data handling, encryption, and access controls, and integrate with your EHR rather than creating a separate, unsecured copy of patient data.

### Where should a dermatology practice start?
Cosmetic-consult follow-up and missed-call recovery typically have the clearest revenue impact, since unconverted consults and unanswered calls are direct, measurable losses.
