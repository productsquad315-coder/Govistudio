# AI Receptionist & Missed-Call Recovery for Dermatology Clinics

An AI receptionist for a dermatology clinic answers or immediately follows up on calls the front desk can't take, and distinguishes a medical skin concern from a cosmetic consultation request so each gets routed and booked correctly. It matters because dermatology front desks juggle two very different call types — urgent medical concerns and elective cosmetic enquiries — on the same phone line, and both get lost when the line is busy.

## How It Works
Input: Inbound calls and voicemails, current availability for medical and cosmetic visit types from the practice's EHR
Processing: The system answers or calls back, identifies whether the call is a medical concern, a cosmetic enquiry, or an existing-patient question, and checks the relevant schedule
Output: A booked appointment, a routed message to the right staff member, or an urgent concern escalated immediately

## Use Cases
- Capturing new-patient cosmetic consultation requests that call during peak enquiry periods
- Booking routine follow-up and skin-check appointments directly against real-time availability
- Distinguishing an urgent lesion concern from a routine cosmetic question and routing accordingly
- Answering common questions (insurance accepted, cosmetic pricing ranges, location) without tying up a staff member
- Logging every call outcome back into the practice record

## Benefits
- Recovers cosmetic consultation calls that would otherwise go to a competing practice or medspa
- Removes the "stuck on one line, missed the next call" problem on a single front-desk phone
- Extends effective office hours without extending staff hours
- Reduces interruptions to clinical staff currently fielding overflow calls

## FAQ
### How does it know a call needs urgent medical attention versus a routine cosmetic question?
The system is built around the practice's own triage criteria for what counts as urgent, and escalates anything matching those criteria immediately rather than attempting clinical judgment itself.

### Can it handle cosmetic pricing questions?
It can share the general pricing ranges and information the practice provides, and route anything requiring a personalized quote to a consultation booking.

### Does it sound like a real person or an obvious bot?
It's built to handle the routine share of calls (scheduling, hours, general questions) smoothly and hand off anything unusual to a human.

### Can it text patients instead of calling them back?
Yes — for cosmetic enquiries especially, a text follow-up often converts better than a voicemail callback, and the system can use either channel depending on practice preference.
