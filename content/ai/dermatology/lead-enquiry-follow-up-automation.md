# Lead & Enquiry Follow-Up Automation for Dermatology Clinics

Lead follow-up automation for a dermatology clinic is a system that follows up automatically on cosmetic consultations that didn't convert to a booked procedure and on new-patient enquiries that went unanswered — instead of relying on staff to remember to call back. It matters because cosmetic consultations are often the highest-margin service a dermatology practice offers, and a consult that isn't followed up on is lost revenue that's already been through the hardest part of the sales process — getting the patient in the door.

## How It Works
Input: Website enquiry forms, cosmetic consultations recorded in the EHR without a booked procedure, missed-call callback lists
Processing: The system applies a follow-up sequence tailored to consultation type and time-sensitivity, and stops automatically once the patient books or responds
Output: Booked procedures, scheduled follow-up visits, or a clearly flagged list of consults that need a personal call

## Use Cases
- Following up automatically on cosmetic consultations (Botox, fillers, laser treatments) that weren't booked on the spot
- Re-engaging website enquiries that never received a response within the first hour
- Working a list of missed-call leads with a structured text/call sequence
- Flagging high-value unbooked procedures for a personal follow-up call rather than an automated message
- Reporting which lead sources and consultation types actually convert to booked procedures

## Benefits
- Recovers cosmetic procedure revenue from consultations that already happened but didn't close
- Removes follow-up from depending on one staff member remembering to do it
- Responds to new enquiries fast enough to matter for time-sensitive cosmetic decisions
- Gives the practice visibility into which consultation types and channels actually convert

## FAQ
### Is this specific to cosmetic services, or does it cover medical follow-up too?
It covers both — medical follow-up (skin-check recalls, treatment follow-ups) and cosmetic consult follow-up, each with a sequence and tone suited to that visit type.

### Does it stop once a patient responds?
Yes. The sequence stops the moment a patient books, replies, or asks to be left alone.

### Can unbooked cosmetic consultations really be tracked automatically?
Yes, if the consultation is recorded in the EHR (Nextech, ModMed, DermEngine) without a follow-up procedure booked, the system can flag it and trigger outreach without a staff member manually building a list.

### Will this feel pushy to patients considering an elective procedure?
The cadence and tone are built with the practice, and it's a timely nudge on something the patient already came in and asked about — not persistent pressure, and it stops on any response.
