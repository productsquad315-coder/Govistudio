# No-Show Prevention & Reminders for Dermatology Clinics

No-show prevention for a dermatology clinic is an automated system that confirms both medical and cosmetic appointments, sends a reminder sequence tuned to each visit type, and fills cancellations from a waitlist. It matters because a missed cosmetic procedure slot and a missed medical follow-up carry different costs and different reasons for going unconfirmed, and a single generic reminder doesn't address either well.

## How It Works
Input: Scheduled appointments from the EHR, patient contact preferences, historical confirmation and cancellation data by visit type
Processing: The system sends a multi-touch reminder sequence, tracks confirmations, and triggers waitlist outreach the moment a slot cancels
Output: Higher confirmation rates, filled last-minute openings, and visibility into which reminder pattern works for medical versus cosmetic visits

## Use Cases
- Running a multi-touch reminder sequence for both medical follow-ups and cosmetic procedure appointments
- Automatically offering a cancelled cosmetic slot to the next eligible patient on a waitlist
- Flagging patients with a pattern of no-shows for a different, more proactive confirmation approach
- Tracking confirmation rates by visit type and reminder channel
- Giving front-desk staff a live view of confirmed versus pending appointments

## Benefits
- Recovers procedure and appointment time that would otherwise sit unbooked
- Reduces the daily manual work of confirming appointments across two very different visit types
- Turns last-minute cancellations into filled slots instead of lost revenue
- Applies a confirmation approach suited to elective, often higher-cost cosmetic bookings specifically

## FAQ
### Do cosmetic procedure no-shows need a different approach than medical visits?
Often yes — elective, higher-cost bookings can benefit from earlier and more deliberate confirmation than a routine medical follow-up, and the system can apply different sequences to each.

### Does this replace confirmation calls entirely?
For most patients, text and automated confirmation covers it. Patients who don't respond, or who have a no-show history, can still be flagged for a personal call.

### Can it fill a cancelled cosmetic slot the same day?
Yes — the moment a slot opens, the system can work down a waitlist by text or call rather than waiting for staff to notice the gap.

### Will patients get annoyed by too many reminders?
The sequence is tuned to the number of touches shown to improve confirmation without becoming noise, and stops as soon as a patient confirms.
