# No-Show Prevention & Reminders for Dental Clinics

No-show prevention for a dental clinic is an automated system that confirms appointments, sends reminders on a schedule proven to reduce missed visits, and fills cancellations from a waitlist — rather than relying on a single reminder call or text the day before. It matters because the average dental no-show rate runs around 15%, and roughly a third of those are simply forgotten appointments that a better reminder sequence prevents.

## How It Works
Input: Scheduled appointments from the practice management system, patient contact preferences, historical confirmation and cancellation data
Processing: The system sends a multi-touch reminder sequence (e.g. one week, two days, and same-day), tracks confirmations, and triggers waitlist outreach the moment a slot cancels
Output: Higher confirmation rates, filled last-minute openings, and a record of which reminder pattern actually works for this specific patient base

## Use Cases
- Running a multi-touch reminder sequence instead of a single day-before text
- Automatically offering a cancelled slot to the next eligible patient on a waitlist
- Flagging patients with a pattern of no-shows for a different, more proactive confirmation approach
- Tracking confirmation rates by reminder channel (text vs. call vs. email) to see what this patient base actually responds to
- Giving front-desk staff a live view of which appointments are confirmed vs. still pending, instead of working from memory

## Benefits
- Top-performing practices confirm around 87% of appointments versus roughly 44% for the average practice — closing that gap is largely a process, not a staffing, problem
- Recovers chair time that would otherwise sit empty on a no-show
- Reduces the daily manual work of calling down the appointment list to confirm
- Turns last-minute cancellations into filled slots instead of lost revenue

## FAQ
### How much does a better reminder sequence actually reduce no-shows?
It varies by practice, but the gap between top-performing practices (around 87% confirmation) and the average (around 44%) shows most of the difference comes from a more disciplined, multi-touch process rather than any single change.

### Does this replace confirmation calls entirely?
For most patients, text and automated confirmation covers it. Patients who don't respond, or who have a no-show history, can still be flagged for a personal call.

### Can it fill a cancellation the same day it happens?
Yes — the moment a slot opens, the system can work down a waitlist by text or call rather than waiting for staff to notice the gap and start calling around.

### Will patients get annoyed by too many reminders?
The sequence is tuned to the number of touches shown to actually improve confirmation without becoming noise — and it stops as soon as a patient confirms.
