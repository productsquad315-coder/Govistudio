# Lead & Enquiry Follow-Up Automation for Dental Clinics

Lead follow-up automation for a dental clinic is a system that follows up on new-patient enquiries and unscheduled treatment plans automatically, instead of relying on staff to remember to call back. It matters because dental practices routinely generate leads — website form fills, missed-call callbacks, treatment plans patients didn't book on the spot — that quietly go cold without a structured follow-up process.

## How It Works
Input: Website enquiry forms, unscheduled treatment plans flagged in the practice management system, missed-call callback lists
Processing: The system applies a follow-up sequence (timing, channel, message) tailored to the type of lead, and stops automatically once the patient books or responds
Output: Booked consultations, scheduled treatment, or a clearly flagged list of leads that need a personal call

## Use Cases
- Following up automatically on treatment plans presented in-chair but not scheduled before the patient left
- Re-engaging website enquiries that never received a response within the first hour
- Working a list of missed-call leads with a structured text/call sequence instead of a one-off attempt
- Flagging high-value unscheduled treatment (implants, ortho consults) for a personal follow-up call rather than an automated message
- Reporting which lead sources actually convert to booked appointments

## Benefits
- Recovers treatment revenue that's already been diagnosed and presented but never scheduled
- Removes follow-up from being dependent on one staff member remembering to do it
- Responds to new enquiries fast enough to matter — most patients call the next practice if the first doesn't respond quickly
- Gives the practice visibility into which marketing channels actually produce booked patients, not just enquiries

## FAQ
### What's the difference between this and a marketing email tool?
This is a system, tied to your practice management data — it knows which specific treatment plan is unscheduled or which specific enquiry hasn't been answered, not just a generic email blast.

### Does it stop once a patient responds?
Yes. The sequence is designed to stop the moment a patient books, replies, or asks to be left alone — it's not a fixed drip campaign that keeps firing regardless of response.

### Can unscheduled treatment plans really be tracked automatically?
Yes, if the treatment plan is recorded in the practice management system (Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental), the system can flag it as unscheduled and trigger follow-up without a staff member manually building a list.

### Will this feel pushy to patients?
The cadence and tone are built with the practice, and the sequence stops on any response — the goal is a timely nudge on something the patient already expressed interest in, not persistent pressure.
