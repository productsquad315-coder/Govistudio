# Recall & Reactivation Campaigns for Dental Clinics

A recall and reactivation system for a dental clinic automatically identifies patients who are overdue for a hygiene visit or haven't been seen in a while, and runs a structured outreach campaign to get them rebooked — instead of relying on a periodic manual recall list pulled by front-desk staff. It matters because hygiene recall is one of the most reliable, highest-margin sources of repeat revenue a practice has, and it's also the easiest to let slip when the front desk is busy with same-day scheduling.

## How It Works
Input: Last-visit and recall-due data from the practice management system, patient contact history and preferences
Processing: The system segments overdue patients (recently overdue vs. long-lapsed), applies a different outreach sequence to each, and books directly into open hygiene slots
Output: Rebooked recall appointments, a reactivated patient list, and visibility into how many lapsed patients came back

## Use Cases
- Running an automatic recall campaign the moment a patient becomes due, instead of a monthly manual pull of the recall report
- Applying a different message and offer to patients overdue by one month versus patients who haven't been seen in two years
- Filling hygiene schedule gaps directly from the recall-due list rather than leaving them open
- Reactivating patients who dropped off after a move, insurance change, or bad experience with a distinct win-back approach
- Reporting recall-to-rebooked conversion so the practice can see the real return on the campaign

## Benefits
- Protects the practice's most predictable, recurring revenue stream from quietly eroding
- Fills hygiene chair time that would otherwise sit open
- Reaches lapsed patients before they establish with a competing practice
- Removes recall management from being a manual, easily-deprioritized front-desk task

## FAQ
### How is this different from the recall reminders built into Dentrix or Eaglesoft?
Built-in recall reminders typically fire a single reminder; this runs a structured, multi-touch sequence that adapts based on how overdue the patient is and whether they've responded, and can book directly into open slots.

### What counts as "lapsed" versus just "due"?
That threshold is set with the practice — typically patients slightly overdue for hygiene get a lighter nudge, while patients who haven't been seen in a year or more get a distinct win-back sequence.

### Can this run alongside our existing recall process?
Yes — it's built around your existing recall-due data in the practice management system, so it strengthens the process already in place rather than replacing the underlying data.

### Does reactivation outreach need to go through a licensed team member?
Routine scheduling outreach doesn't require clinical judgment and can be automated; anything involving clinical advice is routed to the practice's own staff.
