Enquiry Follow-up Kit

Free lightweight tools for clinic owners who lose bookings when enquiries go unanswered or get a late reply. From GoviStudio.

1. Missed-enquiry cost calculator

Plug in weekly volume, miss rate, and average booking revenue. See illustrative weekly / monthly revenue at risk. Runs in the browser — no signup.

Open calculator

2. Follow-up tracker

CSV template for reception to log enquiries; owner runs a Monday review with filters on next action and outcome.
