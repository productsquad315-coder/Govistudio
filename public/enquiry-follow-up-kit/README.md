# Enquiry Follow-up Kit

Free MVP from **GoviStudio** for clinic owners who lose bookings when enquiries go unanswered or get a late reply.

**License:** MIT — see [`LICENSE`](LICENSE)

## What’s included

1. **Missed-enquiry cost calculator** — static page; plug in weekly volume, miss rate, and average booking revenue to see **illustrative** weekly / monthly revenue at risk.
2. **Follow-up tracker** — CSV (and optional TSV) template so reception can log enquiries and the owner can run a Monday review.

All defaults and sample rows are **synthetic**. Numbers are labelled **illustrative** — not GoviStudio measured facts.

## Use it

- Landing: open [`index.html`](index.html) (or this folder’s URL on the site).
- Calculator: [`calculator.html`](calculator.html)
- Tracker: [`docs/tracker-template.csv`](docs/tracker-template.csv) · howto: [`docs/tracker-howto.md`](docs/tracker-howto.md)

## What it is not

Live WhatsApp / phone / form capture, backend analytics, n8n / NocoDB, or multi-branch routing.

## Upgrade path (paid, later)

Enquiry capture from channels you already use, staff assignment, reminders, and simple reporting on reply speed — no pricing or launch date promised here.
